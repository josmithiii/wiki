# SASP - Spectral Audio Signal Processing

Julius O. Smith III, *Spectral Audio Signal Processing*, W3K Publishing,
2011 edition, ISBN 978-0-9745607-3-1.  Free online at
<https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/sasp/>.  Fourth book of the series
([[book-series-overview]]): analysis, processing, and synthesis of audio
signals in terms of spectral representations computed with the FFT.  Its
stated prerequisites are the first two books, [[mdft-book|MDFT]] and
[[filters-book|Filters]].

In this wiki, SASP is the primary source of `spectral_processing/`;
[[spectral_processing/concepts/sasp-overview|sasp-overview]] is the in-wiki
summary of the book's plan.  Page names for deep links:
`jos_books/catalog/sasp.tsv` (616 pages).
