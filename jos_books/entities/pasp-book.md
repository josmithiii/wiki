# PASP - Physical Audio Signal Processing

Julius O. Smith III, *Physical Audio Signal Processing for Virtual Musical
Instruments and Audio Effects*, W3K Publishing, 2010 edition, ISBN
978-0-9745607-2-4.  Free online at <https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/pasp/>.
Third book of the series ([[book-series-overview]]): efficient computational
physical models for delay effects and virtual acoustic instruments.  It
assumes [[mdft-book|MDFT]] and [[filters-book|Filters]].

In this wiki, PASP is the primary source of `waveguide_synthesis/` and a
main reference for `modal_synthesis/` and `virtual_analog/`.  Page names
for deep links: `jos_books/catalog/pasp.tsv` (793 pages).
