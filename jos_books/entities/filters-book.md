# Filters - Introduction to Digital Filters

Julius O. Smith III, *Introduction to Digital Filters with Audio
Applications*, W3K Publishing, 2007, ISBN 978-0-9745607-1-7.  Free online
at <https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/filters/> (`~jos/fp/` serves the same
book; write `filters/` here so the link checker covers it).  Second book of
the series ([[book-series-overview]]): analysis and implementation of
digital filters, assuming [[mdft-book|MDFT]].

No sub-wiki distills Filters, yet nearly every sub-wiki uses it: allpass
and comb sections in reverbs and waveguides, state-space and modal forms in
modal synthesis, the bilinear transform and analog prototypes in virtual
analog, stability and system identification in active noise control.  Page
names for deep links: `jos_books/catalog/filters.tsv` (386 pages).
